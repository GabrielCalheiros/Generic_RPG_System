\chapter{Magic and Optional Rules}

While Eidos default rules simulate a realistic, gritty pre-modern low-fantasy setting, some campaigns call for elements of the arcane, or a more tactical combat experience. This chapter provides a complete, volatile Magic System and optional tactical combat rules that GMs can integrate to customize their table's playstyle.

\section{The Volatile Spellcasting System}

In the world of Eidos, magic is not common, nor is it safe. It is an act of bending the natural laws of reality through intense mental concentration and spiritual devotion. Spellcasting is mentally exhausting and volatile, carrying the risk of magical backlash.

\subsection{Mana Points (MP)}
A spellcaster’s reserve of magical energy is determined by their intellect and the strength of their spiritual conviction. 
\begin{center}
    \textbf{Max Mana Points (MP):} $\text{Mind Modifier} + \text{Adherence Rank Modifier}$
\end{center}

The Adherence Rank Modifier corresponds to the character's spirituality (Chapter 2):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Non-believer / Agnostic:} +0 MP.
    \item \textbf{Casual Adherent:} +2 MP.
    \item \textbf{Orthodox Adherent:} +4 MP.
\end{itemize}

*Spells cost a specific amount of Mana to cast. Spent Mana is fully recovered after 8 hours of uninterrupted sleep.*

\subsection{The Spellcasting Roll}
To cast a spell, the player must spend the required Mana and roll a Spellcasting check:
\begin{center}
    \textbf{Spellcasting Roll:} $2\text{d}20 + \text{MND} + \text{Occultism/Spellcasting Skill}$
\end{center}
The total must meet or exceed the spell's Target Number (TN).

\subsection{Arcane Backlash}
If a spellcasting roll fails, the Mana is still spent, and the volatile energies fracture. The caster must immediately make a **Presence (Willpower)** check:
\begin{center}
    \textbf{Willpower Check:} $2\text{d}20 + \text{PRE}$ vs. \textbf{TN 15}
\end{center}
*   **Success:** The caster safely dissipates the magical energy with no adverse effects.
*   **Failure:** The caster suffers \textbf{Arcane Backlash}. They immediately gain 1 Fatigue Point and suffer the \textbf{Disoriented} status effect (-2 to mental rolls) for 1 hour.

\section{The Spell List}

The following five spells represent the standard domain of low-fantasy magic.

\subsection{Healing Touch}
*   **Mana Cost:** 2 MP
*   **Difficulty:** TN 15
*   **Description:** The caster channels soothing energies through their hands. On a success, the targeted character's remaining critical injury recovery time is immediately reduced by $1\text{d}4$ days (or $1\text{d}4$ weeks if recovering from long-term trauma). This spell can only benefit a single wound once.

\subsection{Aura of Protection}
*   **Mana Cost:** 3 MP
*   **Difficulty:** TN 15
*   **Description:** A shimmering, translucent magical ward surrounds the caster. For 10 minutes, the caster gains a **+2 bonus to passive Defense** and **2 points of Severity Reduction** against all incoming physical attacks.

\subsection{Flame Spark}
*   **Mana Cost:** 1 MP
*   **Difficulty:** TN 10
*   **Description:** The caster shoots a small, intense spark of fire from their fingers. This can light dry wood or torches instantly. In combat, it can be cast at a target within 30 feet. On a hit, it inflicts burning fire damage, forcing a roll on the Impact Critical Injury table (Chapter 7) with a -2 modifier to the roll.

\subsection{Whisper of the Wind}
*   **Mana Cost:** 2 MP
*   **Difficulty:** TN 15
*   **Description:** The caster whispers into the breeze. They can translate a single page of archaic runes or dead languages, or send a silent telepathic message to an ally within 100 yards. The recipient can whisper a telepathic reply in return.

\subsection{Far-Sight}
*   **Mana Cost:** 3 MP
*   **Difficulty:** TN 20
*   **Description:** The caster closes their eyes and projects their consciousness. For 1 minute, they can see and hear from an invisible aerial viewpoint anywhere within 1 mile. The caster's physical body remains completely blind and deaf during this time.

\section{Optional Tactical Combat Rules}

For tables seeking a more mechanical and visually structured combat experience, GMs can introduce the following options.

\subsection{Grid Movement}
*   **Scale:** Standard combat is played on a square grid where **1 square = 5 feet**.
*   **Movement Actions:** Characters can move up to 6 squares (30 feet) as a movement action. Traveling diagonally counts as 1.5 squares (alternating between 5 and 10 feet).
*   **Rough Terrain:** Moving through mud, rubble, or shallow water costs double movement (10 feet per square).

\subsection{Flanking}
Combatants can coordinate their positions to trap an adversary.
*   **Rule:** If two allies are positioned on opposite sides of an enemy (directly across from each other on the grid), they are **Flanking** that target. Both attackers gain a **+2 bonus to all attack checks** against the flanked target.

\subsection{Called Shots}
Instead of rolling randomly for critical injury body regions, attackers can choose to strike a specific area (e.g. aiming directly for the neck or hand).
*   **Rule:** The attacker accepts a **-4 penalty** to their attack check. If the attack succeeds, the attacker chooses which regional Critical Injury table is rolled, bypassing standard regional rolls.
